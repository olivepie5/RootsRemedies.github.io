The origins of this project...


This was an idea I came up with for my EPQ (Extended Project Qualification) because the thought of making a website resonated with me. My A-Level choices are Biology, Maths, Spanish and AS Latin, so I thought it would be useful to corporate my subjects into a personal project. I have always been fascinated by the etymology of words (hence my passion for Latin) and so I thought it would be a good project to create a glossary for A-Level biologists who might also be interested in learning derivations and roots of medical terms. So... Roots and Remedies was created!

I hope this website is beneficial for those who are curious and are fascinated by the work behind words and language evolution.


-Olivia
